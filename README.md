# Markov-Chain-in-Python

Markov Chain is mathematical process that explains the probability of transition of Markov Property from one space to another. It relies specifically on the result of a previous state, meaning that the chain of events that leads to the current state is irrelevant, and the probability of the next transition hinges on the current state of things.

In Python, I demonstrated that via source_text to show the generation of a text based on its memory (source_text) and probabilities of creating a new one based on its memory, cast as keys and their values, putting the theory in the action. Its one form of a generative predictive text.

I hope my program gives you a smooth basic introduction into the process.
